Your evidence cards stay on this device.

The app stores cards in this browser. It has no account or app server.

What is stored

Your draft and saved evidence cards contain only what you enter. That can include observation notes, locality, optional coordinates, candidates, and reference links.

Location protection

New cards use locality-only exports. Coordinates stay out of an export unless you choose Precise and enter both values.

Network activity

The app requests its own files. It does not load analytics, ads, remote fonts, or the recording links you add.

Your controls

  • Export one evidence card as Markdown or CSV.
  • Export saved cards as a JSON backup.
  • Delete cards from Saved cards.
  • Clear site storage in your browser to remove local data.

Reference links

A reference is stored as text. The app does not fetch, cache, or reproduce linked audio.
