Your evidence cards stay on this device.

Cards are stored in this browser. You do not need an account.

What is stored

Cards store your entries, a card number, local timestamps, and review status in this browser. Entries can include notes, locality, optional coordinates, candidates, and reference links.

Location protection

New cards start with locality-only exports. Coordinates are exported only after you choose Precise and enter both values.

Network activity

No analytics, ads, remote fonts, or recording links are loaded.

Your controls

  • Download one card for a spreadsheet or as shareable text.
  • Download a backup of all saved cards.
  • Delete any card from Saved evidence cards.

Reference links

The app stores each reference link as text and does not fetch it.
