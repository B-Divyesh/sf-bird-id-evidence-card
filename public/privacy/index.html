Your evidence cards stay on this device.

Cards are stored in this browser. You do not need an account.

What is stored

Cards store your entries, a card number, local timestamps, and review status in this browser. Entries can include notes, locality, optional coordinates, candidates, and reference links.

Location protection

New cards use locality-only exports. Coordinates stay out of an export unless you choose Precise and enter both values.

Network activity

No analytics, ads, remote fonts, or recording links are loaded.

Your controls

  • Export one evidence card as Markdown or CSV.
  • Export saved cards as a JSON backup.
  • Delete cards from Saved cards.
  • Clear site storage in your browser to remove local data.

Reference links

A reference is stored as text. The app does not fetch, cache, or reproduce linked audio.
