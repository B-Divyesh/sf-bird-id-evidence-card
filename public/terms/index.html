This is a note-taking and comparison tool. It does not identify birds, verify records, or replace field judgment and reporting rules.

Sensitive wildlife

Do not expose nests, roosts, or locations of threatened or persecuted species. Locality-only export is the default, but you remain responsible for reviewing exports before sharing.
