Use evidence cards as notes, not verdicts.

You decide what to log, share, or submit. This tool records the notes you enter; it does not identify birds.

Use carefully

Do not record or share sensitive nest locations. Check licence terms before adding a recording link. Review any export before sharing it.

Your responsibility

Check records against your own observations and the rules of any service you later use. Keep a backup before clearing browser storage or changing devices.

Availability

The app is provided as a free local tool. Browser storage and offline support depend on your browser and device settings.
