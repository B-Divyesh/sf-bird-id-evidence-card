Use evidence cards as notes, not verdicts.

This app records what you observed. It does not identify birds or verify a record.

Your responsibility

Check the accuracy and sensitivity of anything you record or share. Treat app suggestions as candidates. Follow the rules of any service where you later submit a sighting.

Sensitive wildlife

Do not expose nests, roosts, or threatened-species locations. Review each export before sharing it.

Reference media

Only link to recordings you may use. Respect source licences and the terms of services you choose.

No affiliation

BirdNET, Merlin, and eBird may be part of your workflow. This independent tool is not affiliated with them.

Backups and availability

Browser storage may be cleared. Export a JSON backup before changing devices. The software is provided as is.
