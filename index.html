Method, not verdict

A two-minute evidence check

  1. 1

    Write from memory first

    Record shape, movement, habitat, and sound before opening another guide. This limits suggestion bias.

  2. 2

    Name alternatives

    Add at least two candidates when possible. Note one fit and one contradiction for each.

  3. 3

    Compare lawful references

    Link to openly licensed recordings or material you own. Confirm the caller was likely the bird you saw.

  4. 4

    Log at the evidence level

    “Unresolved” is a useful result. Only use “verified” when independent evidence supports the identification.

What this tool does not do

It does not identify birds, download recordings, connect to BirdNET, Merlin, or eBird, or decide whether a record is reportable.
